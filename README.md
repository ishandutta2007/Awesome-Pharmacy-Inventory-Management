# Awesome-Pharmacy-Inventory-Management

## Top Pharmacy Inventory Management Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Pharmacy Inventory, Medication Stock Control, Automated Dispensing, Drug Traceability, Expiry Management & Healthcare Supply Chains*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS/enterprise platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Pharmacy Inventory Management**. These systems help hospitals, retail pharmacies, health systems, warehouses, and pharmaceutical supply chains manage medication inventory, purchasing, dispensing, replenishment, lot and batch tracking, expiry dates, controlled substances, automated dispensing, and inventory analytics.

**Examples** include Omnicell, BD Pyxis, Kit Check, McKesson EnterpriseRx, Swisslog Healthcare, ScriptPro, Parata, BlueBin, Supplylogix, and WaveMark (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with major open-source healthcare inventory, pharmacy management, ERP, hospital information, supply-chain, warehouse, and commodity-management projects. While relatively few open-source products are direct equivalents to proprietary automated dispensing cabinets and robotic pharmacy systems, a strong ecosystem exists for building self-hosted pharmacy inventory platforms using OpenBoxes, OpenMRS, Bahmni, ERPNext, Odoo Community, GNU Health, OpenELIS integrations, DHIS2 supply-chain components, OpenLMIS, and related projects.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

* **[OpenBoxes](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)**
  One of the strongest direct open-source foundations for healthcare and pharmacy inventory management. Supports multi-facility inventory, stock movements, purchase orders, requisitions, shipments, lot tracking, expiry management, reorder points, and healthcare supply-chain workflows.

* **[OpenLMIS](https://github.com/OpenLMIS/openlmis-core)**
  Open-source logistics management information system designed for public-health supply chains. Supports commodity management, requisitions, stock distribution, inventory visibility, and health-system logistics.

* **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**
  Comprehensive open-source ERP platform with inventory, procurement, warehouse, accounting, supplier, manufacturing, and healthcare capabilities. Can be customized into a pharmacy inventory and purchasing system.

* **[Frappe Healthcare](https://github.com/frappe/healthcare)**
  Open-source healthcare application built on the Frappe ecosystem, providing healthcare workflows that can integrate with ERPNext inventory, purchasing, billing, and pharmacy processes.

* **[OpenMRS Stock Management](https://github.com/openmrs/openmrs-esm-stock-management)**
  Open-source stock-management application for healthcare facilities supporting pharmaceuticals and medical supplies, stock receipts, transfers, adjustments, requisitions, stock takes, batch tracking, expiry monitoring, and reorder rules.

* **[OpenMRS](https://github.com/openmrs/openmrs-core)**
  Major open-source electronic medical record platform that provides a foundation for pharmacy, medication, dispensing, and healthcare workflow integrations.

* **[Bahmni](https://github.com/Bahmni)**
  Open-source hospital and healthcare management ecosystem built around OpenMRS and other open platforms. Can support hospital pharmacy workflows and integrates with ERP-style inventory management.

* **[GNU Health](https://github.com/gnuhealth/his)**
  Open-source health and hospital information system supporting clinical, hospital, public-health, and administrative workflows. Its modular ecosystem can be extended for pharmacy and healthcare inventory operations.

* **[GNU Health HMIS](https://github.com/gnuhealth/his)**
  Hospital information-management components within the GNU Health ecosystem that can support pharmacy, medication, warehouse, and clinical logistics workflows.

* **[Odoo Community Edition](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**
  Open-source ERP ecosystem with inventory, warehouse, procurement, purchasing, barcode, accounting, and manufacturing modules. Frequently used as a customizable foundation for pharmacy and healthcare inventory systems.

* **[Odoo Community Association](https://github.com/OCA)**
  Large open-source community ecosystem providing additional Odoo modules for inventory, purchasing, logistics, healthcare-related workflows, accounting, and automation.

* **[iDempiere](https://github.com/idempiere/idempiere)**
  Open-source ERP and business-suite platform supporting inventory, warehousing, procurement, purchasing, accounting, and supply-chain operations.

* **[ADempiere](https://github.com/adempiere/adempiere)**
  Mature open-source ERP platform with inventory, procurement, warehouse, purchasing, supplier, and distribution capabilities suitable for custom pharmacy inventory implementations.

* **[Apache OFBiz](https://github.com/apache/ofbiz-framework)**
  Open-source enterprise application framework providing inventory, order management, procurement, warehouse, accounting, and supply-chain capabilities.

* **[Tryton](https://github.com/tryton/tryton)**
  Modular open-source business platform with stock, purchase, sale, accounting, warehouse, and supply-chain modules that can be adapted for pharmacy inventory management.

* **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**
  Open-source ERP and CRM platform with stock, warehouse, purchasing, supplier, product, barcode, and inventory-management capabilities.

* **[Snipe-IT](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it)**
  Open-source asset-management platform that can complement pharmacy inventory systems when tracking durable medical equipment, devices, scanners, IT assets, and serialized equipment.

* **[Inventree](https://github.com/inventree/InvenTree)**
  Open-source inventory-management platform supporting stock tracking, suppliers, purchasing, locations, parts, BOMs, and traceability. Suitable as a customizable component for pharmaceutical and medical inventory.

* **[PartKeepr](https://github.com/partkeepr/PartKeepr)**
  Open-source inventory-management system for tracking stock, storage locations, suppliers, distributors, and product information.

* **[Grocy](https://github.com/grocy/grocy)**
  Open-source inventory and stock-management system with barcode support, expiry tracking, stock monitoring, and consumption management. Better suited to smaller pharmacies or custom lightweight implementations.

* **[Stockpile-style Community Inventory Projects](https://github.com/topics/inventory-management)**
  The GitHub open-source ecosystem contains numerous smaller inventory-management projects that can be adapted for pharmacy stock tracking, barcode workflows, expiry monitoring, and replenishment.

* **[OpenELIS Global](https://github.com/I-TECH-UW/OpenELIS-Global-2)**
  Open-source laboratory information system that can complement pharmacy and healthcare inventory platforms through laboratory commodity, reagent, and healthcare supply workflows.

* **[DHIS2](https://github.com/dhis2/dhis2-core)**
  Open-source health information platform widely used in public-health systems. Can integrate with supply-chain and logistics systems for commodity reporting and health-program inventory visibility.

* **[SORMAS](https://github.com/hzi-braunschweig/SORMAS-Project)**
  Open-source surveillance and outbreak-management platform that can integrate with health-system supply and logistics infrastructures in public-health deployments.

* **[OpenHIM](https://github.com/jembi/openhim-core-js)**
  Open-source interoperability layer that can connect pharmacy inventory systems with EMRs, laboratory systems, ERP platforms, health-information exchanges, and external services.

* **[OpenFn](https://github.com/OpenFn)**
  Open-source integration and workflow automation platform useful for connecting pharmacy inventory, procurement, EMR, warehouse, laboratory, and public-health systems.

* **[Node-RED](https://github.com/node-red/node-red)**
  Open-source flow-based automation platform useful for integrating barcode scanners, IoT devices, inventory databases, APIs, dispensing equipment, notifications, and pharmacy analytics.

* **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)**
  Open-source analytics and business-intelligence platform that can provide dashboards for stock levels, expiry risk, medication consumption, supplier performance, and inventory forecasting.

* **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**
  Open-source analytics platform suitable for self-hosted pharmacy inventory dashboards, stock-out reporting, expiry analysis, purchasing trends, and operational KPIs.

* **[Grafana](https://github.com/grafana/grafana)**
  Open-source visualization platform that can monitor inventory databases and generate operational dashboards for medication availability, warehouse throughput, and supply-chain metrics.

* **[PostgreSQL](https://github.com/postgres/postgres)**
  Open-source relational database commonly used as a reliable backend for custom pharmacy inventory and healthcare logistics platforms.

* **[OpenSearch](https://github.com/opensearch-project/OpenSearch)**
  Open-source search and analytics platform that can support fast medication, batch, supplier, inventory, and transaction searching across large healthcare supply-chain datasets.

## Additional Strong Open-Source Options

* **Healthcare supply chain**: OpenBoxes, OpenLMIS, OpenMRS Stock Management, Bahmni, and ERPNext Healthcare provide some of the strongest foundations for self-hosted healthcare and pharmacy inventory workflows.

* **ERP and procurement platforms**: ERPNext, Odoo Community, iDempiere, ADempiere, Apache OFBiz, Tryton, and Dolibarr provide purchasing, suppliers, warehouses, stock movements, accounting, and replenishment capabilities.

* **Inventory and warehouse engines**: InvenTree, PartKeepr, Grocy, and community warehouse-management projects can be adapted for smaller or specialized pharmacy environments.

* **Healthcare information systems**: OpenMRS, Bahmni, GNU Health, and OpenELIS can provide clinical, laboratory, patient, and medication context around inventory operations.

* **Public-health logistics**: OpenLMIS and DHIS2-related supply-chain integrations are particularly relevant for government pharmacies, public-health programs, vaccine distribution, and large-scale commodity management.

* **Barcode and RFID integration**: Node-RED, custom Python services, MQTT brokers, PostgreSQL, and open hardware libraries can be combined to build barcode- and RFID-driven inventory workflows.

* **Analytics and forecasting**: Apache Superset, Metabase, Grafana, Python, R, PostgreSQL, and machine-learning frameworks can provide inventory forecasting, expiry prediction, demand analytics, and stock-out monitoring.

* **Interoperability**: OpenHIM, OpenFn, HL7/FHIR libraries, Apache Kafka, and REST APIs can connect inventory platforms with EMRs, pharmacy systems, laboratories, suppliers, and automated dispensing equipment.

* **Pharmacy automation integration**: Direct open-source equivalents to proprietary robotic dispensing systems are limited, but open APIs, Node-RED, ROS-based robotics components, barcode systems, and warehouse-control integrations can provide building blocks for custom automation.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **OpenBoxes** or **OpenLMIS** as the inventory and logistics core, integrate **OpenMRS** or **Bahmni** for clinical and pharmacy workflows, use **ERPNext** or **Odoo Community** for procurement and accounting, add **Node-RED** for barcode/RFID/device automation, and build analytics with **PostgreSQL + Metabase/Grafana/Superset**. For interoperability, connect systems through **OpenHIM**, FHIR APIs, and event-driven integration tools.

## Disclaimer

* This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
* Pharmacy inventory systems may need to comply with pharmaceutical, healthcare, controlled-substance, data-protection, traceability, and regulatory requirements that vary by country and jurisdiction.
* Automated dispensing, medication verification, and clinical decisions should not rely solely on inventory software and require appropriate professional, clinical, and regulatory controls.
* Self-hosted open-source deployments require security hardening, access control, backups, audit logging, data validation, interoperability testing, and regular software updates.
* Open-source inventory platforms are often highly customizable but may require integration work to match the specialized capabilities of proprietary pharmacy robots, automated dispensing cabinets, RFID systems, and medication verification hardware.
